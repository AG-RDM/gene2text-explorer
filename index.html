Transparent scoring

One mean, five inspectable dimensions

DeepSeek V4 Pro scores each dimension in 0.05 increments. Python validates the response and computes the unweighted mean; the model does not choose the final score.

  1. 01

    Biological context

    Organism, tissue, disease, treatment and study objective.

  2. 02

    Cell-type composition

    Represented cell populations and their relative prominence.

  3. 03

    Cell-state similarity

    Activation, differentiation, inflammatory and metabolic states.

  4. 04

    Molecular programs

    Pathways, marker programs and expressed biological processes.

  5. 05

    Gene signatures

    Direct correspondence between the supplied gene-text summaries.

Variant A

Naive top genes

Abundant genes per cell. Direct, but sensitive to housekeeping-gene dominance.

Variant B

Highly variable genes

Per-cell genes restricted to variable features, emphasizing within-dataset variation.

Variant C

Cluster markers

Population-level marker programs. Usually the most interpretable representation.

Variant scores remain separate. They are never combined into a single cross-variant score.
